# AnimusLab Response to the FSB Consultation on AI Governance

We submitted our formal response to the Financial Stability Board's (FSB) consultation on responsible AI adoption in financial services. Our response advocates for a transition from probabilistic runtime guardrails to deterministic policy verification architectures.

## Key Recommendations

1. **Mandating Deterministic Auditing**: Regulators should incentivize institutions to build independent, tamper-evident audit trails that log state transitions.
2. **Decoupled Verification**: Runtime enforcement should be separate from model execution to prevent internal collusion or model bypasses.
3. **Open-Source Policy Mappings**: Financial policy frameworks (like NIST or local regulatory constraints) should be mapped to machine-readable specifications to ensure consistent enforcement.
