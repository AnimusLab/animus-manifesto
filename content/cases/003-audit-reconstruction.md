## 1. Scenario Description

When a compliance failure or anomalous event occurs, institutions must reconstruct the system's exact execution path. Traditional logs can be modified, deleted, or incomplete, leaving auditing agencies without a reliable source of truth.

This case study demonstrates the use of Anchor's Tier 3 append-only local Decision Audit Chain to reconstruct execution states and verify cryptographic transaction signatures.
