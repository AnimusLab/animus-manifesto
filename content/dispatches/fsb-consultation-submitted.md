# Response Submitted to the Financial Stability Board (FSB)

AnimusLab has formally submitted its response to the Financial Stability Board's (FSB) public consultation on the adoption of AI systems in financial services.

## Core Message

Our submission outlines the critical security risks of using purely probabilistic alignment methods to control autonomous AI agents handling capital allocation or transaction execution. We advocate for:
1. **Verifiable Telemetry**: Cryptographically signed audit trails for decision paths.
2. **Decoupled Verification**: Isolating policy verification from model runtime to prevent bypasses.
3. **Standards Integration**: Ingesting regulatory guidelines into deterministic compile-level guardrails.
